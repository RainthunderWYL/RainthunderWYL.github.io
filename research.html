Research Interests

My main research interests involve solar physics, plasma physics, and high-performance computing. The research direction focuses on the Sun-Earth space environment and space weather, aiming to deeply understand the magnetic reconnection processes, fine-scale observational features, and their effects on the space environment during solar eruptive activities through the development of high-precision numerical simulations and advanced analysis methods.
